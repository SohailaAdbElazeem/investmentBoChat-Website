Investor Questions

Why invest in Bo Chat now?

First-mover in the billion-user market: We are the only tech gateway designed specifically for users in emerging markets suffering from slow internet and expensive data. Proven operational efficiency: We achieved a cost per acquisition ($0.18 CPI), record numbers reflecting our ability to scale massively at low costs. Unique technology: We own data-saving technology superior to global platforms, making us a must-have for 2G/3G network users. Cultural & linguistic customization: We fill the gap left by major apps through supporting local dialects and understanding social context in each market. Early growth opportunity: Investing at this stage gives you an advantage before global expansion and projected valuation increase in 2026/2027.

What problem does Bo Chat solve?

We address the data crisis and technical barriers in emerging markets. Current global platforms are heavy and consume expensive internet bundles, designed for advanced phones and 5G networks, leaving millions of users in emerging markets using budget phones and 2G/3G networks with a frustrating experience and high data costs.

What solution does Bo Chat provide?

Our solution is Bo Chat, the first humane social media platform that is highly efficient in data usage. The app is designed to be lightweight yet smart, drastically reducing internet consumption and running smoothly on devices with limited capabilities, while providing advanced communication features based on local interests, languages, and dialects.

Actual Market Size for BoChat App

We target the next billion users in emerging markets, specifically: Budget smartphone users. Residents in areas with unstable or costly internet networks. Targeting users in regions still relying on 2G/3G/4G networks and low-end devices, providing them with the only local alternative that combines modernity and performance efficiency.

Business Plan for Bo Chat Application

Comprehensive platform launch: Complete and launch the web version alongside the mobile app to ensure wider access. Smart content support: Launch an advanced video recording system and AI tools to help creators with minimal resources. Local customization: Expand coverage to include most local dialects in emerging markets to deepen user connection with the platform. Field outreach: Activate the university ambassadors program and ground advertising campaigns in major educational areas. Expansion: Launch targeted marketing campaigns in selected markets in Africa and the Middle East to increase the user base. Strategic partnerships: Contract with telecom companies to provide discounted data packages and enhance the data-saving feature. Economic empowerment: Launch a unique system that allows users in emerging markets to earn monetary rewards for their engagement and activity within the app, turning their time into tangible value. Unbeatable competitive advantage: While global platforms exploit user data, BoChat shares success with users, reducing acquisition costs (CPI) to unprecedented levels.

Legal Entity of Bo Chat Platform

BoChat is operated by Panda Oracle For Information Technology LLC, a global tech entity structured as follows: Headquarters (USA): The company is registered in the US to serve as the strategic and legal center for global management and investment. Operational Center (Egypt): Our operational office in Egypt acts as the heartbeat of operations, responsible for technical development, specialized support for the emerging markets we target, and ensuring accurate understanding of users' cultural and linguistic context.

The shared economy system on the Bo Chat platform

We've built a unique financial system specifically designed for emerging markets, where every second a user spends on the platform is valued. Through our 'earn by watching and posting' algorithm, we allow users to earn direct rewards for their engagement, video sharing, and referrals. This not only fosters user loyalty but also creates a complete economic cycle, making Bo Chat both a source of income and a space for connection.
